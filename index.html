Machine learning from the inside out

What's Under the Hood?

A weekly series on the mathematics, optimisation and numerical methods behind machine-learning algorithms.

Why this series?

Modern machine-learning libraries can hide a substantial amount of mathematics behind a short API call. This series opens that black box: the objective function, the geometry, the numerical method, the implementation choices and the failure modes that matter in practice.

Each public lecture is adapted from a larger private teaching archive and released alongside the LinkedIn newsletter.
